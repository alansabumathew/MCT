Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Mahima Charitable Trust (Reg. No. 74/2012-2013), Bangalore, India ("the Trust", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Donation form: your name, PAN, phone number, email address, donation amount, any note about the cause, and the payment screenshot you upload.
  • Contact and volunteer forms: the details you choose to submit (such as name, email and message).
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to limit spam and repeated submissions.

How we use it

  • To verify your donation and send you a receipt and 80G acknowledgement by email.
  • To maintain donation records as required by Indian tax and charity regulations.
  • To respond to your messages and volunteer requests.

Where it is stored

Donation records are stored in our secured database. Uploaded payment screenshots are stored in a private Google Drive folder owned by the Trust, using Google's Drive API with access limited to files the website creates. Receipts are sent through the Trust's Gmail account. Google's handling of data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the above (such as Google), or when required by law or tax authorities.

Retention

Donation records are kept for as long as required for accounting and statutory purposes. You may ask us to delete other information, such as contact messages.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing us at mctbangalore@gmail.com, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

Security

We use HTTPS and restrict access to donation data to authorised Trust administrators. No online system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
